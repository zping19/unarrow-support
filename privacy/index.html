Unarrow Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Unarrow does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Information We Collect

Unarrow does not require an account and does not collect names, email addresses, contact details, device identifiers, precise or approximate location, photos, contacts, health information, or other personal information.

Local Game Data

The game stores level progress and preferences such as music, sound effects, and haptics locally on your device. This information is used only to restore your game experience. It is not transmitted to the developer or to third parties. You can remove it by deleting the app or clearing the app's local data.

Permissions

Unarrow does not request access to the camera, microphone, contacts, photos, calendar, health data, or location. Device vibration may be used for gameplay feedback when enabled in the game's settings.

Analytics, Advertising, and Third Parties

The current version does not use analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, cloud-storage, authentication, or tracking services. We do not sell or share user information.

Children's Privacy

Because Unarrow does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe information has been provided to us in error, contact us so we can investigate.

Data Security and Retention

No personal information is retained on our servers because the game does not send personal information to us. Local game data remains on the device until the app or its local data is removed.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, privacy laws may provide rights concerning personal data. Unarrow does not maintain a server-side user profile or personal-data record. Questions can be sent to the contact address below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy if the game's features or data practices change. The revised policy will show a new “Last updated” date and will be published at the same public URL whenever possible.

Contact

For privacy questions about Unarrow, email huuuzp@gmail.com.
